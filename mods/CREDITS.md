# Recommended mod pack: credits

All mods are made for Dark Souls Remastered on PC by their authors and published on Nexus Mods. This app converts them for the Switch version
on your own Mac, from your own copy of the game. No file of the game is shipped.

These authors allow re-uploading their files with credit. The files in `included/` are the ones they released, unchanged.
